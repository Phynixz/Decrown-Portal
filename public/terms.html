Legal & Policies

Please read these terms carefully before using the De-Crown Cottage tenant portal.

Last updated: January 2025
01

Acceptance of Terms

By accessing or using the De-Crown Cottage tenant portal, registering for accommodation, or making any payment, you agree to be bound by these Terms of Service. If you do not agree to these terms, please do not use this portal.

These terms apply to all tenants, guardians, and visitors of De-Crown Cottage hostels located at Malete and Ila-Orangun campuses in Kwara State, Nigeria.

02

Eligibility

To register and use De-Crown Cottage facilities, you must:

  • Be a currently enrolled student at the relevant campus
  • Provide accurate and truthful personal information during registration
  • Have a valid guardian or guarantor who can be contacted
  • Be at least 16 years of age or have guardian consent
03

Registration & Room Allocation

Room allocation is done on a first-come, first-served basis per academic session. Submitting a registration form does not guarantee a room until payment is confirmed by management.

A room is only considered yours once your payment receipt has been verified and approved by De-Crown Cottage management.
  • Each room can only be occupied by one registered tenant per session
  • Providing false information during registration may result in immediate eviction without refund
  • Rooms cannot be transferred or sublet to another person
04

Room Retention

Existing tenants may retain their room for the next academic session through the tenant portal. The following conditions apply:

  • A retention request must be submitted before the session deadline announced by management
  • Payment proof must be uploaded through the portal within 7 days of submitting a retention request
  • Your room is not confirmed until management approves your payment
  • Failure to upload payment proof within the stipulated time may result in your room being released to another tenant
  • Retained rooms are subject to the current session's rent amount which may differ from previous sessions
⚠ Submitting a retention request does not guarantee your room. Confirmation only happens after admin approval.
05

Payment

All payments must be made to the official De-Crown Cottage bank account. Payment to any individual or unofficial account is done at your own risk and De-Crown Cottage will not be held responsible.

  • Rent is due at the beginning of each academic session
  • Partial payments are not accepted unless otherwise agreed in writing by management
  • Payment receipts must be uploaded through the portal as proof of payment
  • Management reserves the right to adjust rent amounts between sessions
06

Tenant Conduct

All tenants are expected to:

  • Maintain cleanliness in their room and common areas
  • Respect other tenants and hostel staff at all times
  • Not engage in any illegal activities on the premises
  • Not make structural alterations to the room or property
  • Report any maintenance issues to management promptly
  • Adhere to the hostel's curfew and visitor policy
⚠ Violation of conduct rules may result in eviction without refund and a ban from future registration.
07

Termination

De-Crown Cottage reserves the right to terminate a tenant's accommodation at any time for the following reasons:

  • Non-payment of rent
  • Violation of conduct rules
  • Provision of false information during registration
  • Behaviour that endangers other tenants or staff
  • Damage to hostel property
08

Changes to Terms

De-Crown Cottage reserves the right to update these Terms of Service at any time. Changes will be posted on this page and tenants will be notified through the portal. Continued use of the portal after changes constitutes acceptance of the new terms.

01

Information We Collect

When you register on the De-Crown Cottage portal, we collect the following information:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Academic level
  • Guardian name and phone number
  • Passport photograph
  • Payment receipt / proof of payment
  • Room number and session details
02

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely for the following purposes:

  • Processing your hostel registration and room allocation
  • Verifying your identity and payment
  • Contacting you or your guardian regarding your accommodation
  • Generating your official payment receipt
  • Managing room retention for subsequent sessions
  • Maintaining accurate hostel records
We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third party for marketing or commercial purposes.
03

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely on MongoDB Atlas cloud servers. Uploaded files including passport photographs and payment receipts are stored on Cloudinary's secure servers. We use industry-standard encryption and JWT authentication to protect your account.

  • Passwords are never stored in plain text — they are encrypted using bcrypt
  • All connections to the portal are encrypted via HTTPS
  • Only authorised administrators can access tenant records
04

Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as you are a registered tenant at De-Crown Cottage. Records may be kept for up to 2 years after your last session for administrative purposes.

You may request deletion of your records by contacting management directly. Note that deletion of records will remove your registration history permanently.

05

Your Rights

As a tenant you have the right to:

  • Access your personal information stored on the portal
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your records after leaving the hostel
  • Know how your information is being used

To exercise any of these rights, contact De-Crown Cottage management using the contact details below.

06

Cookies

The De-Crown Cottage portal uses browser storage (localStorage and sessionStorage) to keep you logged in during your session. This is not a tracking cookie and no personal data is shared with third parties through this mechanism.

01

General Refund Policy

De-Crown Cottage operates a strict refund policy. All payments made for accommodation are generally non-refundable. However, refunds may be considered under the following exceptional circumstances:

  • Double payment — if a tenant accidentally pays twice for the same session
  • Room unavailability — if management is unable to provide the allocated room
  • Medical emergency — supported by a valid medical certificate from a recognised hospital
  • School withdrawal — supported by an official letter from the institution
02

Refund Process

To request a refund:

  • Submit a written request to De-Crown Cottage management within 14 days of payment
  • Provide supporting documents for your reason
  • Refunds will be processed within 14 working days of approval
  • Approved refunds will be made to the original payment account
⚠ Refund requests submitted after 14 days of payment will not be considered under any circumstances.
03

Non-Refundable Situations

No refund will be issued in the following situations:

  • Voluntary departure before the end of the session
  • Eviction due to misconduct or violation of hostel rules
  • Change of mind after room allocation is confirmed
  • Failure to move in after payment is confirmed
  • Requests made after 14 days of payment
04

Room Retention Refunds

Payments made for room retention follow the same refund policy as general accommodation payments. If a retention request is cancelled before admin approval, no payment would have been processed yet and no refund is necessary.

Once a retention payment is confirmed and approved by management, it is subject to the general refund policy above.
